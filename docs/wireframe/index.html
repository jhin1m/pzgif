Why PZGIF

Your file never uploads

Decoding and encoding happen in your own browser with WebCodecs and WebAssembly. There is no upload progress bar because there is no upload — and nothing on our side to delete afterwards.

gifski-grade output

Most browser-based GIF tools use a naive encoder and you can see it — banding, dirty edges, dithering noise. We encode with gifski, which builds a smarter palette per frame. Same size budget, visibly better result.

No account, no daily cap

No sign-up wall, no watermark, no "3 free conversions today". Files up to 150 MB on desktop and 50 MB on mobile, as often as you like.

Platform presets

Make a GIF that Discord will actually accept

Discord rejects emoji and stickers over 256 KB, and the error message never tells you how far over you are. Pick a preset, drop your GIF, and PZGIF tunes colors and lossy compression until the file fits — then shows you the exact final size before you download.
