How to convert MP4 to GIF

Drop the video onto this page, drag the two trim handles around the moment you want, choose a frame rate and width, then press Convert. The video is decoded by your browser's own hardware decoder and re-encoded with gifski, so nothing is uploaded and there is no queue to wait in. A three-second clip at 480 px wide typically finishes in under ten seconds on a current laptop.

The two settings that decide your file size are width and frame rate, in that order. A GIF stores complete frames, so doubling the width roughly quadruples the data and doubling the frame rate roughly doubles it. Most clips look right at 12–15 fps and 480 px wide. Save the quality slider for the end.

Keep the clip short

GIF has no motion compression, so length is expensive in a way it never is for video. Two to five seconds is the range where GIF still makes sense. Beyond about ten seconds you are usually better off converting to MP4 or WebP and linking that instead — both play inline nearly everywhere GIFs do, and both are dramatically smaller.

Which formats can PZGIF read?

MP4 and MOV with H.264 or HEVC video, and WebM with VP8, VP9 or AV1 — that covers almost every phone recording, screen capture and download. Unusual containers fall back to a slower path that loads on demand, so the common case stays fast.
